GST Calculator India

What is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is an indirect tax applied to the supply of goods and services in India.

How to calculate GST?

GST can be calculated using the following formula:

GST Amount = Original Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

For example, if the original price is ₹1,000 and the GST rate is 18%:

GST = ₹1,000 × 18 ÷ 100

GST = ₹180

Total = ₹1,180

GST Calculator FAQs

What is 18% GST on ₹1,000?

18% GST on ₹1,000 is ₹180. The total amount including GST is ₹1,180.

What is CGST and SGST?

For an intra-state transaction, GST may be divided between CGST and SGST. For example, an 18% GST rate can be divided into 9% CGST and 9% SGST.

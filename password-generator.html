What is a Password Generator?

A Password Generator creates random combinations of letters, numbers and symbols that can be used as passwords for online accounts.

Why Use a Strong Password?

Strong and unique passwords can make online accounts more difficult to access through password guessing and other common attacks.

Avoid using easily guessed information such as your name, birthday, phone number or simple passwords.

How to Generate a Strong Password?

  1. Select your desired password length.
  2. Choose uppercase and lowercase letters.
  3. Enable numbers and symbols.
  4. Click Generate Password.
  5. Use the Copy button to copy the generated password.

What Makes a Password Strong?

A strong password should generally be long, unique and difficult to guess. Using a combination of different character types can increase the number of possible combinations.

Password Security Tips

  • Use a different password for important accounts.
  • Avoid common passwords.
  • Never share your passwords publicly.
  • Consider using a trusted password manager.
  • Enable two-factor authentication when available.

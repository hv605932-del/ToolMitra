What is a Word Counter?

A Word Counter is an online tool that counts the number of words and characters in a piece of text. It can also provide useful information such as sentence count, paragraph count and estimated reading time.

How to Use the Word Counter?

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. The tool automatically counts your words and characters.
  3. Check sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time.
  4. Use the Copy Text button if you want to copy your text.

Who Can Use a Word Counter?

Students, writers, bloggers, content creators, freelancers and social media users can use a word counter to quickly check the length of their text.

Why Count Words?

Many assignments, articles, applications and online platforms have word or character limits. A word counter makes it easy to check whether your content is within the required limit.

Word Count vs Character Count

Word count measures the number of words in your text, while character count measures individual characters. Character count can include spaces, punctuation and other symbols.

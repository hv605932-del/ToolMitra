What is a Percentage?

Percentage means a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The symbol used for percentage is "%".

Percentages are commonly used for discounts, marks, profits, losses, interest rates, taxes and many other calculations.

How to Calculate a Percentage?

To calculate X percent of Y, use the following formula:

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

Example

To calculate 20% of 500:

(20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 100

How to Calculate Percentage Increase?

Percentage increase can be calculated by finding the difference between the new value and original value and dividing it by the original value.

Percentage Increase = ((New Value - Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

How to Calculate Percentage Decrease?

Percentage Decrease = ((Original Value - New Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Where are Percentages Used?

  • Shopping discounts
  • Exam marks and scores
  • Business profits and losses
  • Bank interest rates
  • GST and taxes
  • Salary increases
  • Price changes

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This page explains how ToolMitra handles information when you use our website.

1. Introduction

Welcome to ToolMitra. We provide free online tools, calculators, utilities and other useful resources. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use our website.

By using ToolMitra, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

ToolMitra is designed to allow users to use many tools without creating an account or providing personal information.

Some information may be provided voluntarily by users, such as information entered into our contact form when contacting us.

3. Information You Enter Into Tools

Some ToolMitra tools allow you to enter text, numbers, URLs or other information in order to perform a calculation or generate a result.

Wherever a tool processes information directly in your browser, that information may be processed locally on your device and may not be sent to our servers.

Users should avoid entering sensitive, confidential, financial or personally identifiable information into tools unless they understand how that particular tool processes the information.

4. Cookies

ToolMitra may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage and support advertising services.

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website.

These advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide advertisements and measure advertising performance, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

6. Third-Party Services

Some ToolMitra features may use third-party services, libraries or APIs to provide specific functionality.

These third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. ToolMitra is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services.

7. External Links

ToolMitra may contain links to external websites. These websites operate independently and may have different privacy policies.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit through a link on ToolMitra.

8. Children's Privacy

ToolMitra provides general-purpose online tools. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of the website and information associated with it. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

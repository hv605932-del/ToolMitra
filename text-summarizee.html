Free Online Text Summarizer

ToolMitra Text Summarizer helps you turn long text into a shorter version containing the most important sentences.

You can use it for articles, study notes, research material, blog content and other text where you need a quick overview.

How Does the Text Summarizer Work?

This browser-based summarizer analyzes the sentences in your text and gives higher importance to sentences containing frequently used meaningful words.

The most relevant sentences are then selected and arranged according to their original position in the text.

How to Summarize Text

1. Paste your text

Copy your article, notes or other content and paste it into the text box.

2. Choose summary length

Choose Short, Medium or Long depending on how much of the original content you want to retain.

3. Generate the summary

Click the Summarize Text button to generate a shorter version of your content.

4. Copy the result

Once the summary is generated, use the Copy Summary button to copy it.

Who Can Use a Text Summarizer?

Students can use summarization to review notes and understand long reading material more quickly.

Writers, researchers, bloggers and professionals can also use summaries to get a quick overview of longer documents.

What Makes a Good Summary?

A useful summary should be significantly shorter than the original text while preserving its main ideas and important information.

Is This an AI Summarizer?

This version of the ToolMitra summarizer uses a browser-based text analysis method rather than a cloud-based AI API. It identifies important sentences from the supplied text.

Does the Tool Upload My Text?

No server upload is required for this summarizer. Processing happens directly in your web browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I summarize an article?

Yes. Paste the article into the text box and choose your preferred summary length.

Can I summarize study notes?

Yes. The tool can help create a shorter version of notes for quick review.

Does the summarizer work offline?

After the page has loaded, the summarization process itself does not require an external API.

How long should my input text be?

Longer text generally gives the sentence-ranking method more information to work with. Very short text may not produce a useful summary.

Is the summary always accurate?

The tool selects important sentences automatically, so you should review the generated summary before using it for important work.

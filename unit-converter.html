What Is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter helps you convert a measurement from one unit to another. For example, you can convert kilometres to miles, kilograms to pounds, or Celsius to Fahrenheit.

Length Conversion

Length conversion is useful for converting units such as kilometres, metres, centimetres, inches, feet, yards and miles.

Weight Conversion

Weight conversion allows you to convert between kilograms, grams, pounds and ounces.

Temperature Conversion

Temperature can be converted between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin depending on your requirement.

Common Unit Conversions

Kilometres to Miles

To convert kilometres to miles, multiply the kilometre value by approximately 0.621371.

Miles = Kilometres × 0.621371

Kilograms to Pounds

One kilogram is approximately equal to 2.20462 pounds.

Pounds = Kilograms × 2.20462

Celsius to Fahrenheit

Celsius can be converted to Fahrenheit using the following formula.

Fahrenheit = (Celsius × 9/5) + 32

Free Online Case Converter

ToolMitra Case Converter is a free online text formatting tool that helps you quickly change the capitalization of your text.

You can convert text into uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case without manually editing every word.

What Is a Case Converter?

A case converter changes the capitalization style of text. It can be useful when formatting documents, headings, social media content, notes, articles and other written material.

Available Text Case Options

UPPERCASE

Converts all letters to capital letters.

Example: hello world → HELLO WORLD

lowercase

Converts all letters to lowercase.

Example: HELLO WORLD → hello world

Title Case

Capitalizes the first letter of each word.

Example: welcome to toolmitra → Welcome To Toolmitra

Sentence case

Capitalizes the first letter of sentences while converting the remaining letters to lowercase.

Example: HELLO WORLD. HOW ARE YOU? → Hello world. How are you?

How to Use the Case Converter

1. Enter your text

Type your text or paste existing text into the text box.

2. Choose a case

Select UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case according to your requirement.

3. Copy your text

After converting the text, click Copy Text to copy the result to your clipboard.

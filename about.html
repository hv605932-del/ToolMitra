About ToolMitra

Simple, useful and free online tools for everyday tasks.

Welcome to ToolMitra

ToolMitra is a free online tools website designed to make everyday digital tasks faster and easier.

Our goal is to provide simple and useful tools that anyone can use without installing complicated software.

What We Offer

ToolMitra provides a growing collection of free online utilities, including:

  • Calculators
  • Text and writing tools
  • AI and content tools
  • Image utilities
  • QR Code tools
  • Productivity tools

Our Goal

Our goal is to create a useful collection of online tools that are easy to understand, quick to use and accessible from both desktop and mobile devices.

Why ToolMitra?

We believe online tools should be simple. You should not have to create an account or install software just to perform a basic calculation or text-related task.

That's why we are building ToolMitra around simple interfaces and practical tools.

Free to Use

Many of the tools available on ToolMitra are provided free of charge. We aim to keep the basic tools accessible to everyone.

Our Future Plans

We plan to continue adding new calculators, productivity utilities, AI-powered tools and other useful online resources.

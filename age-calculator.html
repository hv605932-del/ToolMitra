What is an Age Calculator?

An Age Calculator is an online tool that calculates a person's age based on their date of birth and a selected calculation date.

ToolMitra's free Age Calculator can show your age in years, months and days.

How to Calculate Your Age?

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Select the date on which you want to calculate your age.
  3. Click the Calculate Age button.
  4. Your exact age will be displayed.

Example

Suppose your date of birth is 15 January 2000. Select today's date or any other date and click Calculate Age. The calculator will determine the difference between the two dates.

Why Use an Online Age Calculator?

Manually calculating age can become difficult when months and days are involved. An online calculator makes the process quick and convenient.

You can use this tool for birthdays, forms, applications, eligibility checks and general date calculations.

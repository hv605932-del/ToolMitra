What is a QR Code?

A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that can store information such as website URLs, text, contact details and other data.

Smartphones can scan QR codes using their camera or a QR scanning application.

How to Create a QR Code?

  1. Enter your text, website URL or other information.
  2. Click the Generate QR Code button.
  3. Your QR code will appear below the form.
  4. Click Download QR Code to save the image.

What Can You Put in a QR Code?

  • Website URLs
  • Plain text
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Contact information
  • Social media links
  • Wi-Fi information

Where Are QR Codes Used?

QR codes are commonly used on business cards, product packaging, restaurant menus, posters, advertisements, payments and websites.
